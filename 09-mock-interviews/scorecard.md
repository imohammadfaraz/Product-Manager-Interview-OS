## Overall feedback

**Strongest moment:** What exactly did the candidate do well?

**Biggest gap:** Which missing or weak behaviour most reduced answer quality?

**Decision quality:** Was there a clear recommendation? Was it supported by evidence and trade-offs?

**Clarity:** Could the interviewer follow the logic without guessing what the candidate meant?

**Adaptability:** Did the candidate update the answer when new information appeared?

- What will I practise again?
- What evidence would show that I improved?

## Score interpretation

Do not treat the total as a hiring prediction. Use it to identify patterns. A low score in a role-critical competency deserves focused practice. A high score from a friendly mock should be tested again with a different interviewer and question.

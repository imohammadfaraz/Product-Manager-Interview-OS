- [Further Learning](further-learning.md): Choose additional reading without collecting resources endlessly.

## How to choose external learning material

Before adding a book, course, article, or video to your study plan, ask:

1. Which skill gap will this resource help me close?
2. Does it explain a method I can practise?
3. Is it current enough for the topic?
4. Is the source credible and clear about evidence?
5. Can I turn it into a 20-minute exercise?

Do not spend more time collecting links than practising.

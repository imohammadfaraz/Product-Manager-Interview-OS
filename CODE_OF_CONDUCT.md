# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and constructive learning community.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful of different backgrounds, career paths, communication styles and levels of experience.
- Give specific, actionable feedback focused on the work.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Credit original work and cite sources.
- Keep discussions professional and inclusive.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, sexualised attention, trolling, deliberate misinformation, or publishing another person's private or confidential material are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibility
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Concerns can be raised privately with the repository owner through GitHub.

This code of conduct applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.

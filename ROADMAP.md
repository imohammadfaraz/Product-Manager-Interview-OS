# Repository Roadmap

This roadmap describes intended coverage and maintenance priorities. Targets are not claims about content already present.

## Available in this release
- Interview fundamentals and competency matrix.
- Product Sense, Strategy, Execution, Analytics, Technical PM and Behavioural guides and question banks.
- Ten fictional, data-backed case studies.
- Five mock interview simulations and scoring rubric.
- Reusable answer canvases, worksheets and checklists.
- 7-, 14-, 30- and 60-day preparation plans plus aspiring and senior PM paths.
- Eight AI coaching prompts.
- SQL exercise lab.
- Contribution standards and issue templates.

## Next improvements
### Priority 1: Improve practice depth
- Add worked answers to more question-bank entries.
- Add answer-quality examples at beginner, intermediate and advanced levels.
- Add additional follow-up trees and interviewer calibration notes.
- Expand the glossary with examples and common misconceptions.

### Priority 2: Broaden role and industry coverage
- Add platform, data, AI, developer tools and B2B enterprise scenarios.
- Add APM, growth PM, platform PM, technical PM and product operations paths.
- Add region-aware and India-specific case contexts without presenting assumptions as universal truths.

### Priority 3: Improve learning experience
- Add printable worksheets and progress trackers.
- Add an accessible navigation site if maintainers choose to build one.
- Add SQL dialect notes and optional automated checks.
- Add curated, dated references for company-specific guidance.

## Quality bar for every contribution
A contribution should help a candidate do something: frame a problem, make a decision, analyse data, communicate evidence, or practise a realistic interview. Avoid lists of buzzwords and unsupported claims about what a named company always asks.

# Product Sense Question Bank

Use the prompts as original practice exercises. They are not official or leaked company questions. For each one, timebox yourself, use the Product Sense Playbook, and score your reasoning.

## How to practise each question

- **Beginner:** 15 minutes. Focus on user, problem, and one solution.
- **Intermediate:** 25 minutes. Add prioritisation, trade-offs, and measurement.
- **Advanced:** 35 minutes. Add constraints, multiple stakeholders, rollout, and risks.

### 1. Improve a maps product for daily commuters

- **Level / time:** Beginner to Intermediate / 20-25 min.
- **Tests:** Segmentation, journey analysis, prioritisation, metrics.
- **Task:** Choose one commuter segment and improve one high-friction journey.
- **Strong signals:** You distinguish walking, public transport, two-wheeler, and car contexts; validate a specific friction; avoid feature dumping; define task success and reliability guardrails.
- **Probe:** What if the main issue is inaccurate arrival time rather than route discovery?

### 2. Design a digital service for first-time investors

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Trust, user education, risk awareness, onboarding.
- **Task:** Choose a beginner segment and design a safe first-value experience.
- **Strong signals:** You distinguish education from advice, reduce confusing decisions, measure comprehension and informed completion, and consider regulatory review.
- **Probe:** How would you avoid optimising for risky trading frequency?

### 3. Improve a grocery delivery app for older adults

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Accessibility, user research, prioritisation.
- **Task:** Identify a user need and design a focused improvement.
- **Strong signals:** You avoid assuming all older adults have the same needs; test readability, navigation, support, and payment confidence with users.
- **Probe:** How would you validate the idea without building a separate app?

### 4. Design a product for small retailers to manage inventory

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 30 min.
- **Tests:** B2B segmentation, workflow design, adoption.
- **Task:** Select a retailer segment and define a minimum useful workflow.
- **Strong signals:** You consider owner and staff roles, existing processes, low-connectivity contexts, data entry effort, and repeat value.
- **Probe:** What happens when stock data is inaccurate?

### 5. Improve video discovery for a streaming service

- **Level / time:** Beginner / 20 min.
- **Tests:** User intent, recommendation quality, engagement trade-offs.
- **Task:** Improve the experience of finding something worth watching.
- **Strong signals:** You distinguish discovery from catalogue availability, consider different viewing contexts, and balance watch time with satisfaction.
- **Probe:** How would you detect that recommendations are narrowing user choice?

### 6. Design a public transport companion for a new city

- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** New-market discovery, service constraints, trust.
- **Task:** Design for people unfamiliar with local routes and payment methods.
- **Strong signals:** You identify first-use and repeat-use journeys, data dependencies, language and accessibility needs, offline contingencies, and operational partners.
- **Probe:** What would you launch if live vehicle data is unavailable?

### 7. Improve onboarding for a B2B project management tool

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Multi-persona journeys, activation, time to value.
- **Task:** Improve the path from account creation to a team completing meaningful work.
- **Strong signals:** You distinguish buyer, administrator, manager, and contributor; define activation by completed workflow rather than account creation.
- **Probe:** How do you handle a buyer who signs up but never invites a team?

### 8. Design a service that helps people find local home services

- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Marketplace trust, supply-demand, quality.
- **Task:** Choose a narrow category and design an initial marketplace experience.
- **Strong signals:** You address provider verification, availability, service quality, dispute resolution, and local density.
- **Probe:** What would you do first if there are many customers but few reliable providers?

### 9. Improve a digital payments experience for unreliable connectivity

- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Resilience, trust, error states, technical constraints.
- **Task:** Reduce failed or uncertain payment experiences in low-connectivity contexts.
- **Strong signals:** You separate initiation, processing, confirmation, and reconciliation; design clear pending states; avoid duplicate payments; define recovery and support paths.
- **Probe:** How would you communicate a transaction that is still pending?

### 10. Design a learning product for working adults

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Motivation, habit formation, value delivery, retention.
- **Task:** Help a specific professional segment make progress despite limited time.
- **Strong signals:** You define a concrete learning outcome, fit the product into the user's routine, and measure demonstrated skill rather than lesson views alone.
- **Probe:** What would you change for users who start but do not return?

### 11. Improve search in an e-commerce app

- **Level / time:** Beginner / 20 min.
- **Tests:** Intent, relevance, conversion, quality guardrails.
- **Task:** Improve the ability to find a suitable item.
- **Strong signals:** You distinguish query understanding, result relevance, filtering, inventory, and purchase confidence.
- **Probe:** What if conversion rises but returns also rise?

### 12. Design an AI assistant for customer support agents

- **Level / time:** Advanced / 35 min.
- **Tests:** AI product judgment, workflow fit, evaluation, risk.
- **Task:** Choose one agent workflow where AI could help without removing human accountability.
- **Strong signals:** You choose a narrow task, define human review, source grounding, failure handling, latency and cost, and quality evaluation.
- **Probe:** How do you detect a confidently wrong answer?

### 13. Improve appointment booking for a healthcare clinic

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Service design, accessibility, trust, operational fit.
- **Task:** Reduce friction from finding an available slot to attending the appointment.
- **Strong signals:** You consider patient, clinic, and practitioner needs; cancellations and no-shows; privacy; and offline assistance.
- **Probe:** How would you avoid overbooking?

### 14. Design a feature to help people manage recurring bills

- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** User trust, reminders, financial wellbeing, privacy.
- **Task:** Help users understand and act on recurring expenses.
- **Strong signals:** You avoid dark patterns, make permissions clear, distinguish reminders from payments, and define user-controlled notification settings.
- **Probe:** How do you handle incorrect merchant categorisation?

### 15. Improve the first-week experience of a language-learning app

- **Level / time:** Beginner / 20 min.
- **Tests:** Activation, learning outcomes, motivation.
- **Task:** Help a new learner experience value in the first seven days.
- **Strong signals:** You define a meaningful first milestone, personalise without overwhelming, and use learning progress rather than streaks alone.
- **Probe:** What if users complete lessons but cannot apply the skill?

## Self-review after every answer

- Did I clarify the objective and constraints?
- Did I select a specific user segment?
- Did I distinguish evidence from assumptions?
- Did I identify a meaningful problem before ideating?
- Did I compare options and make a decision?
- Did I define a practical first step?
- Did I choose an outcome and guardrails?
- Did I explain what evidence could change my mind?

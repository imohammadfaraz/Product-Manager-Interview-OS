# 14-Day PM Interview Preparation Plan

**Best for:** Candidates who understand basic PM concepts but need structured practice.  
**Daily commitment:** 60-90 minutes.  
**Output by Day 14:** A role-specific question set, story bank, case practice, and two reviewed mock interviews.

| Day | Focus | Actions | Output |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose | Map the role; take a five-category baseline. | Gap list and target brief. |
| 2 | Interview fundamentals | Read handbook; practise ambiguity and assumptions. | Two structured opening answers. |
| 3 | Product Sense | Solve one design and one improvement prompt. | Two canvases. |
| 4 | User discovery | Practise segmentation, pain points, and validation. | One problem brief and research plan. |
| 5 | Strategy | Solve a strategy prompt and write a decision memo. | One strategy memo. |
| 6 | Market sizing | Complete two bottom-up estimates with sensitivity. | Two calculation sheets. |
| 7 | Execution | Solve a capacity and delivery trade-off case. | Prioritisation canvas and stakeholder update. |
| 8 | Metrics | Build a metric tree and diagnose a drop. | Metric tree and diagnosis worksheet. |
| 9 | Analytics / SQL | Complete four SQL exercises or equivalent data analysis. | Query notes and interpretation. |
| 10 | Technical PM | Explain one system and its failure modes. | One system sketch and trade-off explanation. |
| 11 | Behavioural | Build 8-10 stories and practise follow-ups. | Private story bank. |
| 12 | Case study | Complete one full case from Module 07. | Written recommendation and scorecard. |
| 13 | Mock interview | Complete one 30-minute mock and review it. | Feedback and improvement actions. |
| 14 | Consolidate | Redo weakest question; review company and stories. | Final interview brief. |

## Daily quality rule

Every day must produce an artefact: a written answer, worksheet, story, query, diagram, or recorded explanation. Merely reading a guide does not count as completed practice.

## If you have only 30 minutes on a day

Do one timed question, score it, and write one improvement. Protect the practice habit instead of skipping the day.

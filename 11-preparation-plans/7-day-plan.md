# 7-Day PM Interview Crash Plan

**Best for:** A candidate with an interview in a week who needs focused revision.  
**Daily commitment:** 60-120 minutes.  
**Principle:** Practise the rounds most likely to appear. Do not attempt to master every PM topic in seven days.

## Day 1: Diagnose and target

- Read the [Interview Handbook](../00-getting-started/pm-interview-handbook.md).
- Map the job description using the [JD-to-Interview Map](../08-company-preparation/job-description-to-interview-map.md).
- Complete one baseline question each in Product Sense, Strategy, Metrics, Execution, and Behavioural.
- Score your answers and select the three highest-priority gaps.

**Deliverable:** One-page preparation brief with target role, likely rounds, strengths, and gaps.

## Day 2: Product Sense

- Read the [Product Sense Playbook](../01-product-sense/product-sense-playbook.md).
- Complete one beginner and one intermediate question from the [question bank](../01-product-sense/question-bank.md).
- Use the [Product Design Canvas](../10-frameworks-templates/product-design-answer-canvas.md).
- Record a two-minute recommendation for each case.

**Deliverable:** Two reviewed answers with explicit user segment, problem, choice, and metric.

## Day 3: Strategy and market sizing

- Read the [Strategy Playbook](../02-product-strategy/strategy-interview-playbook.md).
- Complete one strategy question.
- Complete the market-sizing exercise using the [Market Sizing Worksheet](../10-frameworks-templates/market-sizing-worksheet.md).
- Practise explaining assumptions and sensitivity without false precision.

**Deliverable:** One-page strategy memo and one transparent market estimate.

## Day 4: Metrics and execution

- Practise one metric-diagnosis prompt.
- Complete two SQL exercises if relevant to the role.
- Practise one delivery trade-off question.
- Write a stakeholder update for a slipping launch.

**Deliverable:** Metric diagnosis worksheet and concise delivery recommendation.

## Day 5: Behavioural stories

- Build or refine 6-8 stories in your private story bank.
- Cover ownership, conflict, failure, prioritisation, data-led decision, and influence.
- Practise answers in 2-3 minutes.
- Prepare follow-up answers about role, evidence, and learning.

**Deliverable:** A story bank with no exaggerated or unverified claims.

## Day 6: Full mock and company preparation

- Complete one mock interview in the most likely round.
- Ask a peer or AI interviewer to probe your assumptions.
- Research the company using official sources and the [Company Research Worksheet](../08-company-preparation/company-research-worksheet.md).
- Prepare 2-3 thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

**Deliverable:** One scorecard and company brief.

## Day 7: Consolidate and rest

- Review your three biggest mistakes.
- Redo one weak question without notes.
- Practise your introduction and two strongest stories.
- Check interview logistics.
- Stop adding new material. Get adequate rest.

**Deliverable:** A one-page prompt sheet with reminders, not scripts.

## Final interview checklist

- [ ] I can explain why this role and company make sense for me.
- [ ] I have examples that demonstrate the role's core competencies.
- [ ] I can clarify ambiguity and state assumptions.
- [ ] I can make and defend a decision.
- [ ] I can discuss metrics and trade-offs.
- [ ] I can explain my contribution honestly.
- [ ] I have prepared questions for the interviewer.

# AI Weakness Detector Prompt

```text
Act as a pattern-finding coach for Product Management interview preparation.

I will provide multiple anonymised answer transcripts and their questions. Analyse repeated behaviour across answers rather than overreacting to one example.

DATA
[Paste 3-5 question-and-answer pairs.]

ANALYSIS
1. Identify repeated strengths.
2. Identify repeated weaknesses using direct evidence from the answers.
3. Group the weaknesses by competency: framing, customer understanding, business reasoning, structure, data, trade-offs, execution, metrics, communication, and leadership.
4. Distinguish a skill gap from a knowledge gap and a communication habit.
5. Identify whether I jump to solutions, over-clarify, avoid decisions, overuse frameworks, or make unsupported claims.
6. Rank the top three gaps by likely impact on interview performance.
7. Design a five-session practice plan with one measurable output per session.
8. State uncertainty where the sample is too small to conclude.

Do not predict hiring outcomes or infer personality traits. Use observable answer behaviour only.

Return a concise evidence table and a practice plan. Ask for more examples only if the available sample is insufficient.
```

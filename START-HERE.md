# Start Here: Your First 60 Minutes

This guide turns the repository into a usable preparation session. Do not read every file before practising.

## Step 1: Identify your target role (5 minutes)
Write down the role title, seniority, company, product area, job description URL and interview date. If you do not have a target role yet, use Product Manager as a working target and refine it later.

## Step 2: Diagnose your current level (10 minutes)
Open the [competency matrix](00-getting-started/competency-matrix.md). Score yourself from 1 (not yet comfortable) to 5 (can demonstrate this independently) across problem framing, user understanding, strategy, execution, metrics, technical fluency, influence and communication.

Choose the two lowest-scoring competencies. These are your first practice priorities.

## Step 3: Attempt a baseline question (15 minutes)
Choose a question from [Product Sense](01-product-sense/question-bank.md) or [Execution](03-execution-prioritisation/question-bank.md). Set a timer, answer aloud, and record yourself if possible. Do not read a sample solution first.

Use this sequence:
1. Restate the objective.
2. Clarify the user, context and constraints.
3. State assumptions.
4. Structure the problem.
5. Explore options.
6. Make a recommendation and explain trade-offs.
7. Define success and guardrail metrics.

## Step 4: Score the answer (10 minutes)
Use the [mock interview scorecard](09-mock-interviews/scorecard.md). Record specific evidence, not vague statements such as "good communication". Example: "Named two user segments, selected one using frequency and severity, and explained why the other was deferred."

## Step 5: Create your practice plan (10 minutes)
Pick one:
- [7-day sprint](11-preparation-plans/7-day-plan.md) when the interview is imminent.
- [14-day plan](11-preparation-plans/14-day-plan.md) for focused revision.
- [30-day plan](11-preparation-plans/30-day-plan.md) for balanced preparation.
- [60-day plan](11-preparation-plans/60-day-plan.md) for a longer transition.
- [Aspiring PM path](11-preparation-plans/aspiring-pm-path.md) for people entering the profession.
- [Senior PM path](11-preparation-plans/senior-pm-path.md) for leadership and strategy expectations.

## Step 6: Build your evidence library (10 minutes)
Start your [PM Story Bank](06-behavioural-leadership/pm-story-bank-template.md). Capture one real story about ownership, one about conflict, and one about a difficult decision. Never invent achievements or metrics.

## Your daily minimum
A useful daily session can be 45-60 minutes:
- 10 minutes revise one concept.
- 20 minutes answer one question aloud.
- 10 minutes answer follow-up probes.
- 10 minutes score and rewrite.
- 5 minutes log the next practice action.

# PM Competency Matrix

Use this matrix to identify what each competency looks like in practice. Score yourself on evidence, not confidence.

## Scoring scale

| Score | Meaning | Evidence standard |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Not demonstrated yet | You cannot explain a coherent approach or give a relevant example. |
| 2 | Emerging | You know the concept but need significant prompting or miss important steps. |
| 3 | Working | You can apply the skill to a familiar problem with reasonable structure. |
| 4 | Strong | You handle ambiguity, explain trade-offs, and adapt your approach. |
| 5 | Highly effective | You consistently make sound decisions in complex situations and help others improve. |

## Competencies by career level

| Competency | Aspiring / APM | PM | Senior PM | Lead / Group PM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | Clarifies a scoped problem and user | Defines problem and outcome for an area | Selects which ambiguous problems merit investment | Shapes problem portfolio and strategic focus |
| Discovery | Plans basic research and interprets feedback | Combines qualitative and quantitative evidence | Builds discovery across teams and validates strategic bets | Establishes discovery quality and learning systems |
| Product judgment | Compares sensible options | Prioritises user and business value | Makes high-impact trade-offs across teams | Allocates investment across product areas |
| Strategy | Understands product goals | Connects roadmap to strategy | Defines strategy for a product area | Aligns multiple product strategies to company direction |
| Analytics | Chooses basic success measures | Builds metric logic and investigates changes | Uses evidence to guide investment and direction | Creates measurement culture and portfolio visibility |
| Execution | Delivers scoped tasks reliably | Leads cross-functional delivery | Manages dependencies and complex launches | Creates operating mechanisms across teams |
| Influence | Communicates clearly and asks for help | Aligns stakeholders around decisions | Resolves competing priorities across functions | Aligns senior leaders and develops other leaders |
| Leadership | Shows ownership and learning | Leads a problem end to end | Coaches others and raises team performance | Leads through leaders and shapes organisational capability |
| Technical fluency | Understands product and technical basics | Makes informed scope and feasibility trade-offs | Understands architecture and systemic risk | Guides platform and technical investment choices |

## How to use the result

1. Select the three lowest competencies that matter most for the target role.
2. Find one guide and one question bank for each competency.
3. Complete two timed exercises per competency.
4. Ask a peer to score one answer without seeing your self-score.
5. Repeat the baseline after one week.

Do not try to raise every score at once. Improve the highest-impact gaps first.

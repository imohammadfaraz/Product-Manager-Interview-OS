# Case Study: Activation for a team collaboration product

**Category:** B2B SaaS  
**Suggested time:** 30-35 minutes  
**Difficulty:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Scenario status:** Fictional practice case. All organisations, figures, and situations are invented for learning.

## Candidate brief

A fictional B2B collaboration product gets many trial sign-ups, but only a small share of workspaces become active teams. The growth team wants to improve activation.

## Information supplied

Fictional monthly cohort: 10,000 trial workspaces. 75% complete account setup. 48% create a first project. 22% invite at least two teammates. 14% complete a shared workflow within seven days. Workspaces that complete a shared workflow have higher 60-day retention, but larger companies are overrepresented in that group.

## Your assignment

Define activation, locate the bottleneck, and propose an onboarding experiment for a target segment.

## Candidate prompts and hints

Distinguish the buyer, workspace administrator, manager, and daily contributor. Account setup is not necessarily customer value. Explore whether the product has a single-user aha moment or requires team collaboration.

## Facilitator notes: one defensible approach

Define activation as completion of a meaningful shared workflow for the chosen segment, subject to validation. Interview workspace creators and invited users. Test a guided setup that helps a team choose one use case, invite relevant members, and complete one shared task. Consider templates and sample data.

## Suggested success measures

Primary: percentage of eligible workspaces completing a shared workflow within seven days. Leading: invitation acceptance, first project creation, first task completion. Guardrails: setup time, spam invitations, support contacts, and activation differences by company size.

## Follow-up probes

What if users cannot invite colleagues because procurement has not approved the tool? How do you measure activation for a solo consultant?

# Mock Interview 02: Product Strategy

**Candidate time:** 25 minutes  
**Feedback time:** 5 minutes

## Interviewer instructions

Ask the opening prompt. If the candidate requests business context, reveal: the fictional product is a workflow SaaS tool for small professional-services firms, with strong acquisition but uneven retention. Do not volunteer more detail unless asked.

## Opening prompt

"The company wants to expand into a new customer segment. How would you decide whether and where to expand?"

## Follow-up probes

1. Which customer segment would you investigate first?
2. How would you estimate the opportunity without reliable market data?
3. What capabilities would you need to serve the segment?
4. What would make you defer expansion?
5. How would you run a pilot?
6. Which current work would you deprioritise to fund the bet?

## Optional new information

If the candidate asks about retention, reveal: "Some customers use only one workflow and do not invite additional colleagues. Customer interviews suggest implementation effort is a concern."

## Facilitator notes

Look for strategic choice rather than a generic market-entry checklist. Candidate should assess whether the core product is ready, identify segment needs, compare a narrow pilot with broad launch, and establish gates. A defensible answer may recommend expansion or fixing activation and retention first.

## Evaluation focus

Market and customer reasoning, strategic fit, options, capability assessment, and measurable decision gates.

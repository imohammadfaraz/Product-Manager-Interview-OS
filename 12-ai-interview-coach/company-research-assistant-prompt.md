# AI Company Research Assistant Prompt

```text
Act as a research planner helping me prepare for a Product Manager interview at [COMPANY] for [ROLE].

Do not invent current company facts, strategy, product metrics, or interview processes. If you have web access, use current primary sources where possible and provide links and dates. If you do not have web access, tell me what I should verify manually.

RESEARCH TASKS
1. Summarise the company's business model and customer groups, separating verified facts from hypotheses.
2. Identify the product's core user jobs and major workflows.
3. List likely alternatives customers may use, including manual workarounds.
4. Extract the competencies and outcomes from the job description.
5. Suggest one product teardown opportunity, clearly labelled as a hypothesis.
6. Prepare five thoughtful questions for the interviewer.
7. Identify facts that may be outdated and should be checked close to the interview.

For every important claim, include the source or state that it is unverified. Do not present anecdotal interview reports as official process information.

Company: [Company]
Role: [Role]
Job description: [Paste]

Begin by asking me for the job description if I have not provided it.
```

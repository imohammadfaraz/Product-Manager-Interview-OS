# Further Learning: A Focused Resource Strategy

This repository is designed to be self-contained for interview practice. External material can deepen understanding, but reading more is not always the best next step.

## Choose the topic first

| Gap | What to study | Practice output |
|---|---|---|
| Product discovery | Interview methods, observation, synthesis, usability testing | Research plan and insight statement |
| Product strategy | Market structure, business models, competitive advantage | One-page strategy memo |
| Analytics | Metric definitions, cohorts, experimentation, SQL | Metric tree and diagnosis |
| Execution | Delivery planning, dependencies, risk, prioritisation | Decision log and stakeholder update |
| Technical fluency | APIs, data, reliability, security, system design | System sketch and trade-off explanation |
| Leadership | Influence, coaching, conflict, organisational alignment | STAR-L story and follow-up answers |
| AI products | Evaluation, retrieval, tool use, safety, quality, cost | AI product brief and evaluation rubric |

## A simple learning loop

1. Identify one specific knowledge gap.
2. Find one credible learning resource.
3. Take notes on the concepts that change a decision.
4. Apply the concept to a question in this repository.
5. Explain the result in your own words.
6. Stop researching when you can apply and explain the concept reliably.

## Add a resource to this repository

When contributing an external resource, include the title, author or organisation, link, date checked, cost or access caveat where known, and the skill it supports. Do not copy the resource's content. Summarise it in your own words and respect its license.

# 08. Company-Specific Preparation

**Outcome:** You can connect your product judgment and experience to the company's product, customer, business model, and role expectations without relying on rumours or memorised company trivia.

## Work through this module

1. Read the [Company Research Playbook](company-research-playbook.md).
2. Map the [Job Description to Interview Competencies](job-description-to-interview-map.md).
3. Complete the [Company Research Worksheet](company-research-worksheet.md).
4. Prepare a [Product Teardown](product-teardown-template.md).
5. Keep sources and dates for all company-specific claims.

## Research outputs

Before your interview, prepare:

- A concise explanation of the company's customers and value proposition.
- One view on the product's strengths and one opportunity, supported by evidence or labelled hypotheses.
- An understanding of the business model and relevant market dynamics.
- A map from job requirements to your experience and evidence.
- Three thoughtful questions for the interviewer.

**Important:** This module is a research method, not a collection of current company interview claims. Hiring processes and products change. Verify information from current public and official sources.

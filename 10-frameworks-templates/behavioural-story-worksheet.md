# Behavioural Story Worksheet

- Question this story answers:
- Competencies demonstrated:

## Situation

What context and stakes does the interviewer need?

## Task

What were you accountable for? What was outside your control?

## Action

- What did you investigate?
- What options did you consider?
- What did you decide and why?
- How did you align others?
- What did you personally do?

## Result

What changed? Include accurate measures and attribution caveats.

## Learning

What did you learn, and how did your behaviour change afterwards?

## Follow-up rehearsal

- What was the hardest part?
- What did you get wrong?
- Who disagreed?
- What would you do differently?

## Timing

Aim for a 2-3 minute first answer, leaving room for follow-up questions.

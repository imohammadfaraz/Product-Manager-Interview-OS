# 12. AI Interview Coach

Use these prompts with a compatible AI assistant to practise independently. The prompts are model-independent and can be adapted to different assistants.

## What the AI coach should do

- Conduct one question at a time.
- Avoid revealing an ideal answer before you try.
- Ask follow-ups based on what you actually said.
- Challenge vague assumptions and unsupported claims.
- Evaluate reasoning against a consistent rubric.
- Give actionable feedback and a next practice task.

## How to get useful feedback

1. Paste one prompt and provide only the context needed.
2. Answer the question yourself before asking for feedback.
3. Ask the assistant to cite exact phrases from your answer as evidence for its feedback.
4. Reject feedback that assumes facts you did not provide.
5. Verify company, legal, technical, and market facts using reliable sources.
6. Never paste confidential employer, customer, or personal information into a tool unless you are authorised to do so.

## AI limitations

An AI assistant can simulate probing and help organise reflection, but it cannot guarantee how a real interviewer will score you. It may miss domain-specific nuance, invent facts, or give overly agreeable feedback. Treat its output as practice feedback, not a hiring prediction.

## Recommended practice loop

Run one mock, save the transcript privately, ask the evaluator to score it, select one weakness, repeat the answer, and compare the two versions. Focus on the quality of reasoning rather than the score alone.

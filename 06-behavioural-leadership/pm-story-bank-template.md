# PM Story Bank Template

Copy this page once per story. Keep the source notes truthful and private. Do not include confidential customer, employer, or personal information in a public repository.

## 1. Situation

What was happening? What was the customer or business context? What made the situation important?

## 2. Task and ownership

What outcome were you responsible for? What decisions could you make? Which decisions belonged to others?

## 3. Actions and reasoning

- Evidence gathered:
- Options considered:
- Decision made:
- Why this option:
- People involved and how you aligned them:
- Risks or trade-offs:
- What changed during execution:

## 4. Result

- Outcome:
- Metric and definition, if available:
- Time period:
- Attribution caveat:
- Customer or team impact:

## 5. Learning

What did you learn? What would you do differently? Where did you apply the learning later?

## Follow-up questions to rehearse

- What was the hardest decision?
- What did you personally own?
- What evidence changed your mind?
- Who disagreed and why?
- What would you do differently?
- How do you know the result was caused by the work?

## Story quality check

- [ ] I can explain my role without exaggerating.
- [ ] The story has a clear decision, not only a list of activities.
- [ ] I can explain the evidence behind the decision.
- [ ] The result is accurate and properly qualified.
- [ ] I can discuss a mistake or trade-off honestly.
- [ ] The story can be told in 2-3 minutes.

# 60-Day Deep PM Interview Preparation Plan

**Best for:** Career switchers, candidates returning to interviews, or PMs with major competency gaps.  
**Daily commitment:** 45-75 minutes, with one longer weekly session.  
**Approach:** Learn, practise, review, apply, and repeat.

## Weeks 1-2: Build foundations and diagnose gaps

**Week 1**
- Map target role and likely rounds.
- Read the Interview Handbook.
- Complete baseline questions in six categories.
- Build a private practice log.
- Practise two Product Sense questions.

**Week 2**
- Study user segmentation and discovery.
- Complete two product improvement exercises.
- Practise one strategy and one market-sizing question.
- Build the first four behavioural stories.

**Checkpoint:** You can frame a problem, state assumptions, choose a segment, and explain a decision.

## Weeks 3-4: Strengthen product judgment and business thinking

**Week 3**
- Study strategy, competitive analysis, and growth.
- Complete two strategy question-bank prompts.
- Write one strategy decision memo.
- Complete one product case study.

**Week 4**
- Study execution and prioritisation.
- Practise three trade-off scenarios.
- Create a delivery plan and stakeholder update.
- Add stories about conflict, failure, and influence.

**Checkpoint:** You can compare options, explain opportunity cost, and connect a roadmap to a business outcome.

## Weeks 5-6: Build analytical and technical fluency

**Week 5**
- Build metric trees for two products.
- Practise three metric-diagnosis prompts.
- Design one experiment with primary and guardrail metrics.
- Complete SQL exercises appropriate to your role.

**Week 6**
- Study APIs, data, reliability, and system design concepts.
- Explain two product workflows technically.
- Practise one AI product case if relevant.
- Add one story about technical or operational collaboration.

**Checkpoint:** You can interpret a metric change, design a useful measure, and discuss technical trade-offs without pretending to be the engineer.

## Weeks 7-8: Simulate the interview environment

**Week 7**
- Complete two full mock interviews.
- Research target companies.
- Conduct one product teardown per priority company or product area.
- Refine stories and role-specific examples.

**Week 8**
- Complete three mocks across the most likely round types.
- Redo weak questions without notes.
- Practise short, clear recommendations.
- Review interview logistics and prepare interviewer questions.
- Rest before the interview.

**Final checkpoint:** Your mock scores are supported by specific evidence, you can adapt to follow-up questions, and you can explain your decisions with confidence and humility.

## Weekly cadence suggestion

| Activity | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Timed case or question | 4-5 times per week |
| Behavioural story rehearsal | 2 times per week |
| Mock interview | 1 time per week from Week 4 |
| Practice-log review | 1 time per week |
| Rest / catch-up | At least 1 day per week |

Adapt the plan to work, family, health, and other commitments. Consistency matters more than perfect adherence.

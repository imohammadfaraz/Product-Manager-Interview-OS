# Mock Interview 04: Execution and Prioritisation

**Candidate time:** 20 minutes  
**Feedback time:** 5 minutes

## Interviewer instructions

Read the prompt. If asked for constraints, reveal: one engineering team has four weeks of capacity. A security remediation is mandatory and will consume about half the capacity. Two commercial stakeholders have requested separate features for the same release.

## Opening prompt

"You have limited capacity, multiple urgent requests, and a launch date that stakeholders expect you to meet. How do you decide what happens?"

## Follow-up probes

1. What do you clarify before prioritising?
2. How do you separate mandatory work from optional work?
3. How do you compare the two feature requests?
4. What options would you present to stakeholders?
5. What would you communicate if the date or scope must change?
6. How would you prevent the same problem from recurring?

## Optional new information

If the candidate asks about the two feature requests, reveal: "One is requested by the largest customer and is mostly custom workflow work. The other is a broadly requested usability improvement with uncertain impact."

## Facilitator notes

Look for an explicit capacity model, transparent criteria, an understanding of customisation risk, and a realistic option set. The candidate should not simply rank everything with RICE and ignore mandatory security work.

## Evaluation focus

Prioritisation, commercial judgment, stakeholder influence, communication, and delivery realism.

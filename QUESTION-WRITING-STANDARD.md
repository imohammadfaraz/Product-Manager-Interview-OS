# Question-Writing Standard

Use this standard for every new interview question.

## Required question format

### [Question title]
- **ID:** Unique stable identifier, e.g. PS-001.
- **Category:** Product Sense / Strategy / Execution / Metrics / Technical / Behavioural.
- **Difficulty:** Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced.
- **Suggested time:** Minutes.
- **Competencies:** Observable capabilities being assessed.
- **Prompt:** The exact candidate-facing question.
- **Context to clarify:** Important unknowns the interviewer may answer if asked.
- **Candidate task:** What the candidate should produce or decide.
- **Suggested approach:** A sequence of thinking steps, not a rigid script.
- **Follow-up probes:** Questions that test depth, edge cases and trade-offs.
- **Strong-answer signals:** Evidence an interviewer can observe.
- **Common weak patterns:** Errors and why they weaken the answer.
- **Self-review:** Questions the candidate should ask after practice.
- **Related resources:** Links to playbooks or templates.

## Quality principles
1. Questions should resemble decisions PMs make, not trivia quizzes.
2. Avoid hidden constraints that make a question impossible to answer fairly.
3. Distinguish facts from assumptions.
4. Reward prioritisation and explicit trade-offs.
5. Assess reasoning and evidence, not vocabulary.
6. Allow multiple defensible recommendations.
7. Make the intended competency clear to the reviewer, not necessarily to the candidate in a live simulation.

## Difficulty guidance
- **Beginner:** Clear user, goal and constraints; tests core structure.
- **Intermediate:** Ambiguity, multiple segments or competing metrics.
- **Advanced:** Strategic uncertainty, cross-functional constraints, second-order effects or portfolio-level trade-offs.

## Scoring guidance
Use a 1-5 behaviourally anchored scale. A score of 1 indicates missing or unsupported reasoning. A score of 3 indicates a reasonable but incomplete approach. A score of 5 indicates clear, evidence-led reasoning, explicit trade-offs, appropriate adaptation and concise communication. Do not score confidence, accent, personality similarity or verbosity as proxies for competence.

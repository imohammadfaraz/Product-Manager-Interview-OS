# 30-Day Complete PM Interview Preparation Plan

**Best for:** Aspiring and working PMs who want broad preparation with repeated practice.  
**Daily commitment:** 45-90 minutes.  
**Target output:** 20-25 timed answers, 8-12 behavioural stories, 3 case studies, 2 mock interviews, and a role-specific interview brief.

## Week 1: Build the foundation

### Day 1: Define the target
- Read [Start Here](../START-HERE.md).
- Choose a target role and collect the job description.
- Map responsibilities to competencies.
- Complete one baseline question in five categories.

**Output:** Role brief and initial scorecard.

### Day 2: Understand interview expectations
- Read the interview handbook and competency matrix.
- Identify likely interview rounds from recruiter information.
- Practise your introduction in 60-90 seconds.

**Output:** Round map and introduction.

### Day 3: Product Sense fundamentals
- Read the Product Sense Playbook.
- Practise one design prompt.
- Use the Product Design Canvas.

**Output:** One structured design answer.

### Day 4: User understanding
- Practise segmentation and problem framing.
- Take a product you use and map a five-step journey.
- Identify one problem and validation method.

**Output:** Journey map and problem statement.

### Day 5: Product improvement
- Complete two improvement prompts.
- Compare at least two solution options.
- Define outcome and guardrails.

**Output:** Two answer canvases.

### Day 6: Product Sense case
- Complete one case study from Module 07.
- Review facilitator notes only after finishing.
- Record the biggest gap.

**Output:** Case recommendation and scorecard.

### Day 7: Weekly review
- Redo the weakest Product Sense answer.
- Review your practice log.
- Select the next week's three priorities.

**Output:** Week 1 reflection.

## Week 2: Strategy and business thinking

### Day 8: Product strategy
Read the Strategy Playbook and answer one strategy prompt.

### Day 9: Market sizing
Complete two bottom-up estimates. Show formulas, units, and ranges.

### Day 10: Competitive analysis
Compare a product with two alternatives, including a manual workaround. Identify one defensible opportunity.

### Day 11: Growth strategy
Build a funnel or growth loop, locate a bottleneck, and choose one experiment.

### Day 12: Monetisation
Choose a product and identify payer, user, value metric, packaging, and monetisation risks.

### Day 13: Strategy case
Complete the market-entry exercise and write a one-page recommendation.

### Day 14: Weekly review
Give a five-minute strategy presentation. Ask a peer to challenge the assumptions.

**Week 2 outputs:** Two market estimates, one competitive view, one strategy memo, and one reviewed strategy case.

## Week 3: Execution, metrics, and technical fluency

### Day 15: Prioritisation
Compare at least four initiatives. Separate mandatory work from discretionary work.

### Day 16: Delivery planning
Create a phased delivery plan with owners, dependencies, risks, and checkpoints.

### Day 17: Stakeholder management
Practise a conflict scenario and write a concise decision update.

### Day 18: Metrics foundations
Build a metric tree for a product. Define a primary outcome and two guardrails.

### Day 19: Metric diagnosis
Investigate a 15% metric decline. Validate data, segment, rank hypotheses, and recommend action.

### Day 20: Experimentation and SQL
Write an experiment brief and complete three SQL exercises if relevant to the target role.

### Day 21: Technical PM
Explain one system at a high level. Include request flow, failures, reliability, privacy, and cost.

**Week 3 outputs:** One prioritisation decision, one stakeholder update, one metric tree, one diagnosis, one experiment brief, and one system sketch.

## Week 4: Leadership, casework, and interview simulation

### Day 22: Behavioural story bank
Build stories covering impact, conflict, failure, prioritisation, influence, and learning.

### Day 23: Leadership follow-ups
Practise questions about ownership, disagreement, difficult decisions, and mistakes.

### Day 24: Company research
Complete the Company Research Worksheet using official and current sources.

### Day 25: Product teardown
Analyse one core journey and prepare a five-minute point of view.

### Day 26: Full product or strategy mock
Run one mock interview under a timer. Score it and repeat the weakest section.

### Day 27: Metrics or execution mock
Run a second mock in the round most relevant to the target role.

### Day 28: Case study under pressure
Complete a new case without notes. Practise adapting when the interviewer adds a constraint.

### Day 29: Final revision
Review your three most important gaps, story bank, company brief, and questions for the interviewer.

### Day 30: Readiness check
Repeat the baseline questions from Day 1. Compare scores and evidence. Build a short revision plan for any remaining weak areas.

## Weekly review questions

- Which skill improved and what evidence shows it?
- Which mistake repeated across answers?
- Which assumption did I fail to state?
- Did I make a decision or only describe possibilities?
- Which practice should I stop, start, or continue?

## Readiness criteria

You are better prepared when you can answer common prompts with a clear structure, defend decisions under follow-up, explain your personal contribution, and identify what evidence could change your view. A calendar completion percentage is not a substitute for demonstrated ability.

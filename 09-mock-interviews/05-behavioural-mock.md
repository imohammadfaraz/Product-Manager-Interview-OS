# Mock Interview 05: Behavioural and Leadership

**Candidate time:** 20 minutes  
**Feedback time:** 10 minutes

## Interviewer instructions

Ask each question in sequence. Give the candidate up to three minutes per initial answer. Probe for role clarity, decision-making, and learning. Do not coach during the answer.

## Questions

1. Tell me about a time you had to say no to a stakeholder.
2. Tell me about a decision you made with incomplete information.
3. Describe a failure or missed outcome and what you changed afterwards.
4. Tell me about a time you influenced a team without authority.

## Follow-up probes

- What was your specific responsibility?
- What options did you consider?
- What evidence informed your decision?
- What did the other person care about?
- What would you do differently now?
- How did you know the outcome improved?

## Facilitator notes

Look for truthful, specific stories with clear personal ownership. Strong candidates explain the choice and trade-off, not only the activity. Watch for vague "we" statements that obscure the candidate's contribution, unsupported metrics, and stories where the candidate blames others.

## Evaluation focus

Ownership, influence, learning, judgment, communication, and credibility.

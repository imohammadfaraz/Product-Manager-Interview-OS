# Product Strategy Question Bank

These are original practice prompts. They are not claims about questions used by any particular employer.

### 1. A product has strong user growth but weak revenue growth. What strategy would you recommend?
- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Business model, segmentation, funnel diagnosis, monetisation judgment.
- **Task:** Identify plausible causes and prioritise what to investigate before changing pricing.
- **Strong signals:** Separates acquisition mix, activation, usage, conversion, pricing, and retention; considers customer value and cost to serve.
- **Probe:** What if revenue per paying customer is rising but paid conversion is falling?

### 2. Should a regional food-delivery business enter a new city?
- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Market entry, supply-demand density, operations, unit economics.
- **Task:** Recommend entry, pilot, or defer and define launch gates.
- **Strong signals:** Addresses local demand, restaurant supply, delivery coverage, acquisition, operational quality, and repeat behaviour.
- **Probe:** Which side of the marketplace would you seed first?

### 3. How would you define a product vision for a fragmented B2B workflow?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Synthesis, customer understanding, strategic focus.
- **Task:** Turn a broad opportunity into a clear future state and choices.
- **Strong signals:** Connects a real customer problem to differentiated value and a sequence of capabilities.
- **Probe:** How would you know the vision is too broad?

### 4. A competitor launches a feature customers are requesting. What do you do?
- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Competitive judgment, customer evidence, prioritisation.
- **Task:** Decide whether to match, differentiate, partner, or ignore.
- **Strong signals:** Investigates which customers need it and why; considers strategic fit and opportunity cost.
- **Probe:** What if your largest customer threatens to leave?

### 5. How would you monetise a free productivity tool?
- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Packaging, willingness to pay, value metrics.
- **Task:** Propose a testable monetisation model.
- **Strong signals:** Identifies payer and user, value metric, upgrade trigger, cost to serve, and risks to trust or adoption.
- **Probe:** How would you avoid surprising existing users?

### 6. A mature product has slowing growth. Where would you look for its next growth opportunity?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Growth diagnosis, portfolio thinking, strategic options.
- **Task:** Build a growth opportunity map and select one bet.
- **Strong signals:** Separates market saturation from execution issues, considers new segments and increased value for existing customers.
- **Probe:** How would you protect the core business while testing a new segment?

### 7. Should a company build an AI assistant, buy a third-party solution, or partner?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Build-buy-partner, differentiation, technical and operational risk.
- **Task:** Recommend a path for one defined workflow.
- **Strong signals:** Evaluates data rights, reliability, control, cost, latency, integration, vendor dependence, and strategic learning.
- **Probe:** What if the vendor changes its pricing or model behaviour?

### 8. A B2B product has high adoption among administrators but low adoption among daily users. What is the strategy?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Multi-persona value, product-led growth, organisational adoption.
- **Task:** Identify why the buyer's decision is not translating into daily value.
- **Strong signals:** Separates purchase, setup, invitation, activation, and repeated workflow completion.
- **Probe:** What if administrators are satisfied and do not see a problem?

### 9. How would you decide whether to expand an existing product into an adjacent customer segment?
- **Level / time:** Intermediate / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Segment selection, capability fit, opportunity cost.
- **Task:** Build an evidence plan and staged decision.
- **Strong signals:** Compares need, reach, willingness to pay, product gaps, sales motion, and retention potential.
- **Probe:** What is the smallest test that would change your mind?

### 10. A marketplace is growing transactions but quality is declining. What strategic choices do you have?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Marketplace dynamics, trust, quality-growth trade-offs.
- **Task:** Recommend how to balance growth with trust and service quality.
- **Strong signals:** Distinguishes supply quality, matching, incentives, fraud, fulfilment, and dispute resolution.
- **Probe:** What if tightening standards reduces supply by 20%?

### 11. How would you prioritise between serving a large customer and improving the core product for many customers?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 25 min.
- **Tests:** Strategic focus, revenue concentration, product integrity.
- **Task:** Evaluate the request and recommend a response.
- **Strong signals:** Separates reusable capability from one-off customisation; considers revenue risk and roadmap cost.
- **Probe:** What if the customer represents 30% of revenue?

### 12. What would you do if a product's primary distribution channel became unavailable?
- **Level / time:** Advanced / 30 min.
- **Tests:** Strategic resilience, channel diversification, prioritisation.
- **Task:** Create a response plan for the next quarter and longer term.
- **Strong signals:** Quantifies exposure, protects existing customers, tests alternatives, and considers dependency reduction.
- **Probe:** Which short-term action would you not take?

## Strategy answer review

- Did I state the decision and time horizon?
- Did I distinguish facts, assumptions, and unknowns?
- Did I identify a specific customer segment?
- Did I assess alternatives rather than defend the first idea?
- Did I explain capability and resource requirements?
- Did I include milestones, risks, and measurable gates?

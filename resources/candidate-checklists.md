# Candidate Checklists

## Before you begin preparing

- [ ] I know the target role, level, and product area.
- [ ] I have saved the job description.
- [ ] I know the interview rounds that have been communicated to me.
- [ ] I have identified my three highest-priority skill gaps.
- [ ] I have a repeatable practice schedule.

## Before every practice question

- [ ] I understand the objective.
- [ ] I know which user or stakeholder I am discussing.
- [ ] I have identified the constraints.
- [ ] I have set a timebox.

## During an answer

- [ ] I clarify material ambiguity.
- [ ] I state assumptions and distinguish them from facts.
- [ ] I structure the problem before jumping to solutions.
- [ ] I compare options and make a decision.
- [ ] I explain trade-offs and risks.
- [ ] I define success and guardrails where relevant.
- [ ] I adapt when the interviewer provides new information.

## Before the actual interview

- [ ] I can explain my motivation for the role.
- [ ] I have prepared relevant, truthful stories.
- [ ] I have researched the company using current sources.
- [ ] I have a product point of view supported by evidence or labelled hypotheses.
- [ ] I have prepared questions for the interviewer.
- [ ] My technology, audio, location, and calendar details are ready.
- [ ] I have a small prompt sheet, not a memorised script.

## After the interview

- [ ] I recorded the topics and questions while they were fresh.
- [ ] I noted where I was unclear or unsupported.
- [ ] I identified one improvement for the next interview.
- [ ] I sent any appropriate follow-up communication.
- [ ] I kept confidential details private.

# 11. Preparation Plans

Choose a plan based on the time available and your current skill level. A plan works only when you practise actively. Reading ten guides without producing answers is not preparation.

| Plan | Best for | Weekly effort |
|---|---|---|
| [7-day crash plan](7-day-plan.md) | Interview scheduled soon | 60-120 min/day |
| [14-day focused plan](14-day-plan.md) | Basic foundation or refresher | 60-90 min/day |
| [30-day complete plan](30-day-plan.md) | Balanced, comprehensive preparation | 45-90 min/day |
| [60-day deep plan](60-day-plan.md) | Career transition or major skill gaps | 45-75 min/day |
| [Aspiring PM path](aspiring-pm-path.md) | No formal PM title yet | Flexible, project-based |
| [Senior PM path](senior-pm-path.md) | Senior, Lead, or Group PM roles | Flexible, evidence-heavy |

## How to adapt a plan

1. Use the competency matrix to identify the most important gaps.
2. Replace low-priority topics with requirements from the target job description.
3. Keep at least two timed mock sessions in the final week.
4. Leave time for rest and reflection. Do not add new frameworks the night before an interview.
5. If you miss a day, continue from the next high-value task. Do not abandon the plan to catch up mechanically.

## Daily session structure

- 10 minutes: revise one concept.
- 20-35 minutes: answer a question under a timebox.
- 10 minutes: score the answer.
- 5-15 minutes: repeat the weakest section.

Record your work in a personal practice log. Do not publish private employer or customer information.

# Mock Interview 01: Product Sense

**Candidate time:** 25 minutes  
**Feedback time:** 5 minutes

## Interviewer instructions

Read the opening prompt. Let the candidate clarify the objective. If the candidate asks for a product context, say it is a consumer app that helps people plan and complete everyday tasks. Do not provide a target segment unless asked. Use the probes below one at a time.

## Opening prompt

"How would you improve a task-management app for people who struggle to keep up with personal and work commitments?"

## Follow-up probes

1. Which user segment are you choosing and why?
2. What evidence would you gather before committing to a solution?
3. You have identified three problems. Which one will you solve first?
4. The team can ship only a small change in four weeks. What would you test?
5. How would you know the change creates real value rather than more app usage?
6. What could go wrong for users who already manage tasks differently?

## Optional new information

If the candidate asks about user research, reveal: "In a small discovery study, some users say they capture tasks in multiple places and forget to review them. The sample is small and not necessarily representative."

## Facilitator notes

Look for a candidate who narrows the user and context, treats the discovery signal as a hypothesis, distinguishes capture from planning and completion, and proposes a focused test. Strong answers may focus on capture friction, review rituals, prioritisation, or cross-tool visibility if the candidate justifies the choice.

Do not reward a long feature list. Ask what the user would do differently and how the team would observe it.

## Evaluation focus

Problem framing, user empathy, solution prioritisation, test design, and measurement. Use the scorecard.

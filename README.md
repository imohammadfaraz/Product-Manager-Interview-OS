# PM Interview OS

An open-source, action-driven Product Manager interview preparation system. Learn the skill, practise with realistic prompts, evaluate your reasoning, and close your gaps.

> Learn how to think. Practise how to solve. Demonstrate how to lead.

## Start here
Read [START-HERE.md](START-HERE.md), choose the path that matches your experience, and use the [30-day plan](11-preparation-plans/30-day-plan.md) if you have time to prepare.

## What's inside
- 13 guided modules covering fundamentals, product sense, strategy, execution, metrics, technical design, behavioural leadership, cases, company research, mocks, templates, preparation plans and AI coaching.
- Original practice questions with competency notes and follow-up prompts.
- 10 fictional, data-backed product cases.
- 5 interviewer-led mock simulations and a reusable scorecard.
- 10 reusable canvases and worksheets.
- 8 model-independent AI coaching prompts.
- SQL practice exercises with sample schema, data and worked solutions.

## Recommended practice loop
1. **Learn:** Read one playbook and write down the key decision steps.
2. **Attempt:** Answer one question aloud before looking at guidance.
3. **Probe:** Answer follow-ups and explain assumptions and trade-offs.
4. **Evaluate:** Score yourself with the [mock scorecard](09-mock-interviews/scorecard.md).
5. **Improve:** Rewrite the weakest part and repeat the question 48 hours later.

Do not memorise model answers. Use examples to understand the quality of reasoning expected, then build answers from your own experience and assumptions.

# Behavioural & Leadership Question Bank

Prepare truthful stories. Do not invent achievements or inflate your ownership. When you lack workplace experience, use relevant examples from internships, education, volunteering, personal projects, or community work and explain the context honestly.

### 1. Tell me about a product decision you are proud of.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min answer plus probes.
- **Tests:** Ownership, judgment, impact.
- **Task:** Explain the problem, options, decision, and result.
- **Strong signals:** Clear personal contribution, evidence, trade-off, and honest attribution.
- **Probe:** What would you do differently now?

### 2. Tell me about a time you disagreed with Engineering.
- **Level / time:** PM and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Collaboration, technical judgment, influence.
- **Task:** Show how the disagreement was resolved.
- **Strong signals:** Understands the engineering concern and reaches a reasoned decision without dismissing expertise.
- **Probe:** What if Engineering's recommendation had been right?

### 3. Describe a time you said no to a senior stakeholder.
- **Level / time:** PM and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Prioritisation, communication, courage.
- **Task:** Explain the request, alternatives, and relationship outcome.
- **Strong signals:** Uses customer or business reasoning and offers a constructive alternative.
- **Probe:** Did the stakeholder accept the decision?

### 4. Tell me about a failure or missed goal.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Accountability, learning, resilience.
- **Task:** Own the part that was yours and explain the change.
- **Strong signals:** Specific mistake, impact, correction, and evidence of learning.
- **Probe:** What signal did you miss earlier?

### 5. Tell me about a time data changed your mind.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Analytical thinking, intellectual honesty.
- **Task:** Explain the original belief, new evidence, and revised action.
- **Strong signals:** Shows how evidence was evaluated and decision changed.
- **Probe:** What if the data was incomplete?

### 6. Describe a time you influenced without authority.
- **Level / time:** PM and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Influence, empathy, alignment.
- **Task:** Show how you created shared commitment.
- **Strong signals:** Understands stakeholder incentives and creates options.
- **Probe:** Who remained unconvinced?

### 7. Tell me about a time you had to make a difficult prioritisation decision.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Trade-offs, focus, communication.
- **Task:** Explain what was selected and what was deferred.
- **Strong signals:** Explicit criteria, opportunity cost, and follow-through.
- **Probe:** What evidence would cause you to revisit it?

### 8. Describe a product launch that did not go as planned.
- **Level / time:** PM and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Execution, risk, learning.
- **Task:** Explain preparation, response, impact, and prevention.
- **Strong signals:** Transparent ownership and customer-centred recovery.
- **Probe:** What would you change in the launch checklist?

### 9. Tell me about a time you used customer feedback to change a roadmap.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Discovery, synthesis, strategic judgment.
- **Task:** Show how feedback was validated and translated into action.
- **Strong signals:** Does not treat one loud request as universal evidence.
- **Probe:** How did you resolve conflicting customer needs?

### 10. Describe a time you simplified a process or product.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Simplification, impact, execution.
- **Task:** Explain what was removed or changed and why.
- **Strong signals:** Identifies complexity and measures the resulting improvement.
- **Probe:** What trade-off did simplification introduce?

### 11. Tell me about a time you managed multiple urgent priorities.
- **Level / time:** All levels / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Focus, capacity, communication.
- **Task:** Explain the prioritisation and stakeholder communication.
- **Strong signals:** Distinguishes urgent from important and sets expectations early.
- **Probe:** What did you deliberately not do?

### 12. Describe a time a team member or partner was struggling.
- **Level / time:** Senior and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Coaching, empathy, leadership.
- **Task:** Explain how you supported accountability and progress.
- **Strong signals:** Balances support, clarity, and ownership.
- **Probe:** How did you know the intervention helped?

### 13. Tell me about an unpopular decision you made.
- **Level / time:** Senior / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Judgment, courage, communication.
- **Task:** Explain decision quality and how you handled disagreement.
- **Strong signals:** Evidence-led reasoning and willingness to revisit if facts change.
- **Probe:** What did you learn from the reaction?

### 14. Describe a time you inherited a poorly defined problem.
- **Level / time:** PM and above / 3 min.
- **Tests:** Ambiguity, discovery, ownership.
- **Task:** Show how you moved from ambiguity to an actionable decision.
- **Strong signals:** Clarifies objective, maps unknowns, and creates a learning plan.
- **Probe:** What assumption was most dangerous?

### 15. Why do you want to be a Product Manager, and why this role?
- **Level / time:** All levels / 90 sec.
- **Tests:** Motivation, self-awareness, role understanding.
- **Task:** Connect experience and interests to the role's actual work.
- **Strong signals:** Specific motivation, relevant evidence, and realistic understanding of the job.
- **Probe:** Which part of PM work do you find most challenging?

## Build your story coverage

Map each question to a story in your private story bank. Aim to have at least two stories for high-priority competencies. Practise adapting the story to different prompts without changing the underlying facts.

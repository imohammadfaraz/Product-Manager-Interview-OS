## How to use the banks
1. Pick one category based on the role's likely interview loop or your weakest score.
2. Choose a question without reading the guidance first.
3. Timebox your answer.
4. Use the linked playbook only after your first attempt.
5. Write down the strongest follow-up you struggled with.
6. Repeat the same question after 48 hours and compare the evidence in your answer.

For realistic practice, use the [mock simulations](09-mock-interviews/README.md) and [scorecard](09-mock-interviews/scorecard.md).

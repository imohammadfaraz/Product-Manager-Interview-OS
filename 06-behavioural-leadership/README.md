# 06. Behavioural & Leadership

**Outcome:** You can tell credible, concise stories that show what you owned, how you made decisions, how you worked through difficulty, and what you learned.

## Work through the module

1. Read [STAR-L Storytelling](star-l-storytelling.md).
2. Build your [PM Story Bank](pm-story-bank-template.md).
3. Practise [Influence and Conflict](influence-and-conflict.md).
4. Answer the [Behavioural Question Bank](question-bank.md).
5. Use the [Behavioural Story Worksheet](../10-frameworks-templates/behavioural-story-worksheet.md).

**Your deliverable:** 8-12 truthful stories that cover different competencies and can be adapted to follow-up questions.

# Launch Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to identify gaps before a launch. Not every item applies to every release. Record why an item is not applicable when risk is material.

## Customer and product

- [ ] Target users and eligibility are clear.
- [ ] Core journey and edge cases have been tested.
- [ ] Value proposition and user messaging are clear.
- [ ] Accessibility and localisation have been considered.
- [ ] Pricing, terms, or policy changes are communicated.

## Engineering and quality

- [ ] Critical functionality is tested.
- [ ] Monitoring and alerts are ready.
- [ ] Failure and rollback paths are documented.
- [ ] Data migration and compatibility are checked.
- [ ] Security and privacy reviews are complete where needed.

## Operations and support

- [ ] Support teams understand the change.
- [ ] FAQs, runbooks, and escalation paths exist.
- [ ] Sales / customer success enablement is ready where relevant.
- [ ] Partner or vendor dependencies are confirmed.

## Measurement

- [ ] Primary metric is defined.
- [ ] Guardrails are defined.
- [ ] Instrumentation is validated.
- [ ] Baseline and segments are recorded.
- [ ] Review owner and time are set.

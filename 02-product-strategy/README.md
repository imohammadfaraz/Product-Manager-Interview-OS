# 02. Product Strategy

**Outcome:** You can assess a market or product opportunity, choose where to compete, define a differentiated value proposition, and connect strategic choices to measurable outcomes.

## Complete this module

1. Read [Strategy Interview Playbook](strategy-interview-playbook.md).
2. Practise market sizing with [Market Sizing Guide](market-sizing-guide.md).
3. Use the [Strategy Decision Memo](../10-frameworks-templates/strategy-decision-memo.md).
4. Answer the [Strategy Question Bank](question-bank.md).
5. Review [the market-entry exercise](market-entry-exercise.md).

## Your deliverable

Write a one-page strategy recommendation containing: target segment, problem or opportunity, market context, strategic choice, alternatives rejected, capabilities required, risks, milestones, and success measures.
